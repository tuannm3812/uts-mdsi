```text
Project Preferences Online Submission will open soon

Dear All,

Please follow the instructions to submit your project preferences through our online system:

Project Preferences.

The system will open today, 27 July at 2:00 PM.

The deadline for submitting your project preferences is Tuesday, 28 July at 11:59 PM.

Before the preference submission deadline, you are allowed to:

Re-submit your group preferences.
Change the order of your preferences (e.g., if you initially preferred Project A over Project B, you can update your preferences to place Project B above Project A).
Project allocation will be conducted based on the rule "First in, first served."

Please note that I have added one more project to the available project list. We may also add additional projects today or tomorrow if more project opportunities become available.

Please continue to check the project list before finalising your preferences.

Best regards,

Ali Anaissi

Steps to Be Completed

Step 1: Form a Group
You are strongly recommended to form a group (minimum of 5 and maximum of 6 members) before selecting a project. A well-organized group of 6 members will be given priority based on the first-in, first-allocated rule. Any incomplete groups cannot be guaranteed to work on their top three preferred projects.

Example:

Group A (6 members) submits their online form on 28 July at 13:10, selecting Project 1, Project 2, and Project 3.
Group B (fewer than 5 members) submits their online form on 28 July at 13:10, selecting Project 1, Project 2, and Project 4.
Since Group A is complete, they will have a higher priority for Project 1 than Group B.

A discussion board is available on Canvas to help students find teammates with similar project interests before the semester starts.

If you are unable to form or join a group before project selection, please proceed directly to Step 2.

Step 2: Submit Your Project Preferences
The project list Download project list is already available on Canvas.

Review the project list and select your top three preferred projects.
Submit your preferences via the online form, which will be available in Week 1 (27 July). The link will be sent via a Canvas announcement.
If you are not part of a group, please enter your own three project preferences in the online form. We will try to match you with a group based on your selections. However, we cannot guarantee that you will be assigned to your preferred project.

Step 3: Project Allocation Results
Projects will be assigned based on the first-in, first-allocated rule, your preferences, and project availability.

The project allocation results will be available on Canvas by the end of Week 1.

Good Luck

Ali Anaissi
```

## Confirmed decisions

- Preference system opened at **2:00 PM on 27 July 2026**.
- Deadline: **11:59 PM on 28 July 2026**.
- Groups contain **5–6 students**.
- A complete group of six receives priority over an incomplete group submitting at the same time.
- Each student/group ranks three projects.
- Preferences may be resubmitted and reordered before the deadline.
- Allocation considers submission order, group completeness, preferences, and availability.
- Students without a group may submit individually but are not guaranteed a preferred project.
- Results are expected by the end of Week 1.
- The official list updated on 28 July contains 16 projects. Projects 15 and 16 are the latest additions.
