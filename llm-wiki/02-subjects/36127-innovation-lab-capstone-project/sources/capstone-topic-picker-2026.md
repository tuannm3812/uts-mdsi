## Recommended preference strategy

### Balanced top three

1. **C14 — Organisational Knowledge Credibility**
   - Strongest match for enterprise AI, RAG, knowledge graphs, calibration, and explainability.
   - The brief explicitly states that multiple student teams are welcome, reducing allocation risk.
2. **C15 — Custom Terminal-Bench Harness**
   - Excellent fit for agent development, Python, testing, reproducible evaluation, and software engineering.
   - Newly added and likely to attract strong interest because coding agents are a current LLM focus.
3. **C11 — Grounded NCC AI Assistant**
   - Strong graph/RAG alternative with prepared structured data, starter code, tests, and clear evaluation criteria.

### Alternative agentic-AI ordering

Use **C14 → C15 → C16** if the group strongly prefers coding-agent research and accepts the additional infrastructure, model-access, token-cost, and benchmark-reproduction risks of Projects 15 and 16.

### Allocation tactics

- Form a complete group of **5–6**, ideally 6, as quickly as possible.
- Agree on one preference order before submitting.
- Submit early; allocation is first-in, first-served and incomplete groups are not guaranteed a top-three choice.
- Recheck whether resubmission changes the effective submission time before reordering an existing entry.
- Prepare a one-paragraph rationale and tentative role split for C14, C15, and C11 so the group can begin immediately after allocation.
- Keep C10 and C16 as discussion-stage alternatives, but remember the form accepts only three preferences.
