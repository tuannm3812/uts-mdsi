## Candidate formulations to test against the literature

These are hypotheses for exploration, not final topics:

1. **Transformer direction:** Determine whether a time-series transformer improves fire-hotspot prediction over interpretable tree-based and recurrent baselines under temporal and spatial holdouts.
2. **Graph direction:** Represent spatial regions and their environmental relationships as a graph, then evaluate whether a spatiotemporal graph model improves hotspot prediction and regional generalisation.
3. **Transparency direction:** Compare whether region-specific explanations, calibrated confidence, and uncertainty estimates provide more reliable actionable insight than a single black-box prediction model.
4. **Fusion direction:** Evaluate which fusion of climate, weather, vegetation, land-use, and spatial-context features produces robust gains without introducing leakage.

The final project should likely combine one primary modelling contribution with transparency and robust validation, rather than attempting all four directions.

## Recommended scope discipline

A feasible project may use:

- One clearly defined target
- One geographic resolution
- One prediction horizon
- Two or three simple baselines
- One advanced model family
- One explainability approach
- One uncertainty or calibration analysis
- Spatial and temporal validation
- A concise decision-oriented interpretation

Avoid committing to both a full transformer study and a full GNN study until the literature, data, and compute requirements are understood.
