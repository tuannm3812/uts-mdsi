## Full meeting questionnaire

### Research question and contribution

- What is the exact research question?
- Are we predicting fire occurrence, susceptibility, severity, or another target?
- What would count as the novel research contribution?
- Is there an existing baseline, paper, or previous student project that I should extend?
- Would the intended outcome be methodological research, an applied decision-support system, or both?

### Scope

- What geographic area should the project cover?
- What spatial unit should be used: grid cells, bioregions, LGAs, grazing regions, or something else?
- What prediction horizon is expected: days, weeks, months, or seasons?
- Is the scope achievable within one semester?
- What features are essential, and what should be treated as optional?

### Data

- Which satellite, climate, landscape, and historical-fire datasets will be provided?
- Is there an existing clean dataset, or is data acquisition and integration part of the research contribution?
- Are there licensing, access, storage, or preprocessing constraints?
- How are fire and non-fire observations labelled?
- Is any sensitive, restricted, or partner-owned data involved?

### Methods

- Which baseline models are expected?
- Should I investigate tree-based ML, spatiotemporal models, deep learning, graph ML, or another approach?
- Is SHAP the preferred explainability method?
- Should uncertainty, probability calibration, and sensitivity analysis be included?
- How should spatial and temporal leakage be prevented?
- What validation design is expected for unseen regions and future periods?

### Skills and resources

- How much GIS and remote-sensing knowledge is required before starting?
- Which tools should I learn: GeoPandas, Rasterio, Xarray, QGIS, Google Earth Engine, or others?
- Will university computing, cloud credits, or an existing environment be available?
- Is Kaggle or Colab acceptable for model experiments?
- Are there researchers or domain experts involved who can help with environmental interpretation?

### Supervision and deliverables

- How often would supervision meetings occur?
- What should I prepare before the first formal meeting?
- What milestones would you expect during the semester?
- What code, report, maps, models, and reproducibility artefacts are expected?
- If results are strong, could the work be developed into a preprint or publication?
- How would authorship and work after the subject be handled if a manuscript is developed?

### Administration

- What information must be sent to the subject coordinator to confirm the topic and supervisor?
- Is a formal topic description or supervisor-approval email required?
- Are ethics, data agreements, or special approvals needed?
- What is the deadline for confirming supervision and enrolment?
