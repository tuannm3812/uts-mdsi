# 36126 Research Project Topic Picker — Spring 2026

This clean list contains only topics from the two official files in the **36126 Innovation Lab Research Project** Google Drive folder. It contains **27 research topics**. Capstone-only topics have been removed.

## Research topics

### Tony Huang — visualisation and human–AI interaction

| ID | Topic | Concise summary | Main methods |
|---|---|---|---|
| R01 | **Infographic Intent Language: Translating Communication Goals into Design Decisions** | Design an intermediate language that converts communication goals into transparent, controllable infographic choices. | LLMs, specification design, interface prototype, user evaluation |
| R02 | **Can Large Language Models Critique Infographics as Well as Humans?** | Compare multimodal-LLM judgements of infographic quality with human judgements. | Multimodal LLMs, benchmark design, human ratings, statistics |
| R03 | **Evidence-Grounded Conversational Infographic Design with LLMs** | Build a conversational workflow that verifies evidence before recommending infographic content and layouts. | RAG, LLMs, fact verification, interface design |
| R04 | **Translating Natural-Language Graph Design Prompts into Layout Transformations** | Convert requests such as “separate clusters” into deterministic graph-layout operations. | LLM parsing, NetworkX/Graphviz, graph drawing |
| R05 | **Building and Evaluating an Intelligent Assistant for Node-Link Diagram Design** | Recommend graph layouts using graph analytics and research evidence, then test whether the assistant improves design and learning. | RAG, graph profiling, layout algorithms, controlled/user study |
| R06 | **Investigating Whether Humans and AI Understand Node-Link Diagrams in the Same Way** | Test whether people and multimodal LLMs respond similarly to crossings, labels, paths, and community separation. | Controlled experiments, graph tasks, multimodal LLM evaluation, statistics |
